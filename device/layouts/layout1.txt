* Hotkeys 1-8: tap sends F13-F20 (focus workspace N), hold >250ms sends a
* second key (move the focused window to workspace N silently). The hold keys
* are F21-F24, INTL5, INTL2, =MAC, INTL4, because a hold action must be a
* single key and those are the unused codes the firmware can emit. Every hold
* key must have a keysym in a us keymap: INTL1 (KEY_RO) and INTL3 (KEY_YEN)
* have none, and on Hyprland 0.56.2 a keysym-less press fires every
* unmodified code: bind at once. Hyprland binds all sixteen by keycode in
* ~/.config/hypr/bindings.lua. 8 of the layout's 10 tap-and-hold slots.

[hk2]>[f14][t&h250][f22]
[hk3]>[f15][t&h250][f23]
[hk4]>[f16][t&h250][f24]
[hk5]>[f17][t&h250][intl5]
[hk6]>[f18][t&h250][intl2]
[hk7]>[f19][t&h250][=mac]
[hk8]>[f20][t&h250][intl4]
